# ADR 0206: Wiring the skill lanes' services into the app

- Status: Accepted (code merged on main; status updated 2026-10-06)
- Date: 2026-10-01
- Owner: P15-A (Shell and IA)

## Context

Lanes C (Find a time), D (Pick a place), and E (chaining, audit, Swap photos) merged with requests for the shell: `docs/requests/P15-C.md`, `P15-D.md`, and `P15-E.md`. Each request says what to build and call. A few choices about how are the shell's, and are recorded here.

## Decision

1. **A skill whose flag is off runs no service.** `AppModel` drops the service of every skill not in the build's `SkillFlags`, so a friend's message can never reach it, even though the agent card already leaves it out. Swap photos is built (P15-E 4.8) and stays off this way until its flag turns on.
2. **Services read the owner's choices live.** Find a time asks, each time, whether it is on, whether to use the calendar, and the standing rules (P15-C request 2). `OwnerChoices` answers from the settings and rules in memory, which change before they are saved, so turning a skill off or choosing Just ask me takes effect at once. Before settings load, it answers off, Just ask me, and no rules.
3. **One EventKit store and one location access per app.** Each permission sheet asks through the same object the skill reads from, and only after Continue (ADR 0013 decision 3). Debug builds use the real calendar and location alerts; only photos stays simulated while Swap photos is off.
4. **What left your phone is journaled on disk.** Lane E's `EgressRecorder` is the Outbox observer, fanned out with lane G's audit log, over `FileEgressJournal` (`egress-journal.json`): each change is written before it returns, and an unreadable file or failed write throws, so the send stops. The coordinator is the `EgressSink` and returns only once the record is saved. At launch the order is: load the interactions, finish `recover()`, then restore the services, so no service can schedule a send before recovery (privacy review of PR #73). Until recovery finishes, every audit reads as incomplete, and every caller of startup waits for the same startup task. After that, completeness is live, not a snapshot: each send is marked pending in observable app state in `willSend`, before the transport sees it, and cleared only once its record is durably on the interaction, so a send the transport took and then failed keeps its plan's audit incomplete for the rest of the launch (focused review of PR #73). A skill's send whose interaction the coordinator has not installed yet (a friend's request answered right after it was announced) stays pending and is written once the interaction is installed; only a link-level send, or one recovered from the journal with no interaction left, is unattributed. Plan detail says it cannot confirm a conversation that is still pending, and shows no Kept list at all when the journal was unreadable (P15-E 4.1).
5. **After-plan-ends links start while the app is open.** `PlanEndScheduler` runs from launch and is checked again on foreground. The coordinator confirms each decision with `ScheduledChain.isCurrent` against the stored link in the same main-actor step, and a start goes through its single writer and is flushed before the service hears of it (ADR 0011 amendment 13). The local notification at the next plan end is deferred until Swap photos is on, since nothing can opt in before then.
6. **Retirements are retried at launch and on foreground** for any service that keeps them (Swap photos), through a small `RetriesRetirements` protocol the app conforms lane E's service to.
7. **Local state for ADR 0011 amendments 16 and 17 lives in the plan notes file** (ADR 0204), never sent. Cards the owner passed stay hidden there until their skill reports `ownerPassed`, so a relaunch does not bring them back. A quiet ask becomes one initiator interaction per friend, each in its own conversation, started separately; the coordinator gives them a local group ID, recorded before any starts, and Home shows siblings still in progress as one request while each match gets its own card.
8. **Down for... runs in Debug only, and the on-device model everywhere.** Lane B's service needs a PSI provider, and the only one is `InsecurePSIStub` in StarlingFakes, so Release registers `DownFor.descriptor` but runs no Down for... service, as ADR 0144 decision 3 directs, until a private provider exists. Debug runs it on the stub with its requests in `down-for-requests.json` (interaction-store protection). Release and Debug both pass `FoundationModelsAgent` as the `SkillModel`, which Release had lacked.
9. **The model's names are read as lane B documents them** (P15-B request 2): with `everyoneExcept` they are the friends left out, and if one cannot be told apart New asks nobody until the owner picks; a name that is one of the owner's groups asks that group.
10. **A send's named interaction owns its sheet and its audit** (P15-B request 8): the coordinator trusts `OutboundContext.interaction` when the interaction is in the send's conversation or belongs to the send's skill, for consent and for egress records alike.
11. **Pair plans can become one plan.** When two or more of a quiet ask's requests are plans, Home offers to invite those friends together; the tap opens New in Invite mode, and the invite joins the quiet ask's local group, which ends the offer (P15-B request 9).
12. **How long a request stays open depends on the skill** (the owner's device test, 2026-10-02). A skill whose `IntentSchema.asksForExpiry` is true (Down for...) uses the owner's choice, shown as "Open for 3 hrs" under the control "How long friends can answer". One whose value is false (Find a time, Pick a place) shows no such chip: its request stays open until the time window it asks about starts, at least one day and at most seven days from now, and a chained request stays open until its parent plan starts. It is how long friends can answer, never the plan's length.
13. **Every chip in New is applied and edits in place.** The composer describes each chip (`ComposeChip`): the part of the request it is, how a tap edits it, and whether it can be removed. Optional chips can be removed; a skill's required slots can be edited but not emptied. Down for...'s activity appears only on its skill chip, so no chip repeats it. The modes read "Ask quietly" and "Ask directly", shown only for a skill with both.
14. **New stays usable while it reads, and re-reads only what changed** (device test 2, issue #95). Start is never off without a line right under it saying why. New re-reads the words only after a 0.8 second pause and only when they changed beyond case and spacing; a re-read keeps every chip the owner edited or removed, follows the new words for the rest, and keeps a skill picked from the tiles. While a re-read runs, Start stays on with the chips shown, and the new reading applies only if the owner has not touched the draft since. A friend whose Starling has not said hello yet is still asked, and New says it is waiting to hear from them; the Inbox loop rereads the friends list when an unknown peer appears, at most every two seconds, so a fresh friend's first hello is kept.
15. **The Edit sheet holds this request's details; standing preferences stay in You › Your rules.** What, when, where, group size (for a skill that asks), the most to spend, who, how friends are asked, and how long friends can answer. Find a time's when is a range of days, optionally only some hours of each (one window over the days and a daily window: the request's own hours, such as a meal's, or 5 PM to 10 PM for evenings). With no days in the words it is lane C's default, `FindATimeDefaults.timeConstraints` (the next 7 days, in a meal's hours for a meal; P15-C request 6a). The expiry chip shows the time friends can answer until, never a length.

## Consequences

- A skill lane's service is added in one place per build (`LiveServices`, `DebugHarness`), and its descriptor comes from its package, never a copy.
- Turning on Swap photos needs only its flag, its picker in Needs you (P15-E 4.9), and the plan-end notification.
