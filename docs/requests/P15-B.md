# Requests from lane P15-B (Down for... skill and the model)

## 1. Remove Phase 1 Down once the app runs Down for... (Orchestrator, lanes A and F)

**What.** When lane A wires `DownForService` in place of `DownNegotiator`, and the simulator's Down integration tests (`Tools/Simulator/Tests/DownIntegrationTests`) move to it:

- Core: remove `DownService`, `DownIntent`, `DownLevel`, `DownMatch`, `DownEndReason`, `DownEvent` (`Packages/StarlingCore/Sources/StarlingCore/Down.swift`) and `StarlingFakes.ScriptedDownService` (`DownFakes.swift`). Keep `IssueKey.downLevel` only if a Phase 1 build still needs to decode; nothing in Down for... sends it.
- `Capability.down` can go with them: Down for... reads support from `AgentCard.skills`.
- P15-B then removes `DownNegotiator` and its profile, conversation, and configuration from `StarlingNegotiation` in a follow-up PR (ADR 0211).

**Why.** ADR 0010's consequence: the facade stays until lane B moves Down into its skill package, then it goes. Two Down implementations invite fixes to the wrong one.

**Meanwhile.** Nothing breaks: `DownNegotiator` is unchanged and still tested.

## 2. Wiring Down for... in the app (lane A)

**What.** The app composes the service like this:

```swift
let relay = DownForConsentRelay(wrapping: consentCoordinator)    // before the Outbox
let outbox = Outbox(transport: secureLink, policy: policy, consent: relay, observer: egress)
let downFor = DownForService(localPeer: me, outbox: outbox, model: agent, psi: psi, store: persistentRequestStore)
relay.attach(downFor)
```

- Feed every `InboxEvent` to `handle(_:)`, including `hello`, so unsupported friends are left out by card.
- Call `restore(_:)` at launch with the store's Down for... interactions. Provide a persistent `DownForRequestStore` (the in-memory one forgets on restart); its records hold the owner's rules, so keep them in the same protected storage as the interaction store.
- Show `DownFor.revealNote` ("If nobody's up for it, nobody sees you asked.") on the compose screen, and `DownForService.unsupported(among:cards:)` for "Maya's Starling doesn't do this yet".
- Fall back to `ProposalTemplate.sentence(_:now:)` whenever `SkillModel.proposalText` throws (model unavailable, or the sentence failed its checks).

**Why.** ADR 0210 decisions 14 to 17.

## 3. Attributing a member's sends to its interaction (lane A, lane E, Orchestrator)

**What.** A member of someone else's group sends in that starter's conversation (ADR 0210, Consequences), so looking an interaction up by `Envelope.conversation` misses those sends: the egress observer, and the coordinator when it applies a pass for a declined sheet, would not find the member's interaction. Today the service offers `DownForService.interaction(for: ConversationID, peer: PeerID) -> InteractionID?`.

Proposed Core change, so every skill and the observer work the same way: add `interaction: InteractionID?` to `OutboundContext` (local only, never encoded), set by every skill service on every send, and passed to the policy's `Disclosure` the same way. The `OutboxObserver` and the coordinator then attribute by it.

**Why.** "What left your phone" must equal what the consent sheet showed (ADR 0011, decision 5). Without this, a member's egress is recorded under no interaction.

**Meanwhile.** `interaction(for:peer:)` on the service.

## 4. Outbox sequence numbers across a restart (Orchestrator)

**What.** A restarted app's `Outbox` starts every conversation at sequence 0, and a friend's `Inbox` drops those frames as replays until the sequence passes the highest it saw. `LifecycleTests.aRequestResumesAfterARestart` shows it: the first three PSI frames after the restart were dropped, and only retries recovered. Proposal: seed a conversation's first sequence from the clock (for example milliseconds since 1970), which keeps sequences rising across restarts without storing anything; `Inbox` needs no change.

**Why.** ADR 0210 decision 17 resumes a request in the same conversation, as `Interaction.conversation` requires. Any skill that resumes after a restart hits this.

**Meanwhile.** Retries cover it, at the cost of a slower first exchange.
