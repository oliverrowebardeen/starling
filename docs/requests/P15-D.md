# Requests from lane P15-D (Pick a place)

Item 10 was settled by Core v2.1. One policy question is open (item 12). Everything else is for other lanes, through the Orchestrator. The package is `Packages/Skills/PickAPlace`, with products `PickAPlace` and `PickAPlaceMapKit`. ADRs 0230 to 0232 explain the decisions.

## 1. Lane A (shell): location permission

- **What.** Add `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` to the app's Info.plist (App/project.yml) with the text of `PickAPlaceSkill.locationPurpose`:

  > Starling uses your location only while it finds places near you. Your location stays on your iPhone; friends see only the places you suggest.

  Show Starling's pre-permission sheet from `PickAPlaceSkill.locationSheet` (title, the three rows, one Continue button, and the note after Don't Allow).
- **Why.** ADR 0013, decision 6: lane D owns the location purpose string, lane A the app and the sheet. Without the key, `requestWhenInUseAuthorization()` shows no prompt.
- **Meanwhile.** Tests use `FakeLocation`; nothing in the package asks at launch.

## 2. Lane A: Compose for Pick a place

- **What.**
  - An area field, or Nearby.
  - Call `PlaceFinder(search: MapKitPlaceSearch(), location: CoreLocationAccess())`:
    - `.find(what, near:)` first.
    - On `.needsLocationPermission`, show the sheet; Continue calls `.allowLocationAndFind(what)`.
    - On `.manualEntry(reason)`, let the owner type places with `PlaceCandidate.manual(_:)`.
  - Before the owner can send, check `PickAPlaceSkill.askable(candidates, limits:)` with the intent's limits. If it is empty, keep the owner in Compose with one line ("None of these fit your limits").
  - Stage the chosen candidates with `StagedCandidates.stage(_:for:)` under the interaction's ID, apply `.started`, then call `start` (ADR 0011, amendment 13). The service never emits `.started`. If `start` still throws (`noCandidates`, `nothingFitsYourLimits`), the coordinator applies `.failed`.
- **Why.** Candidates must exist before anything is sent, and the permission must come from Starling's sheet, never from the service (ADR 0232).

## 3. Lane A: composing the service

Build `PickAPlaceService` once, with:

- `localPeer`: the Outbox transport's `localPeer`;
- the app's single `Outbox` and `PairedPeerStore`;
- the `StagedCandidates`;
- `MapKitPlaceSearch()`;
- `ledger: UserDefaultsPickAPlaceLedger()`, which keeps what must survive a relaunch: admission times, the organizer's deadlines, and pending withdrawals (ADR 0230);
- `conversations:` the same `ConversationLedger` the app passes to `Outbox(ledger:)` (ADR 0021);
- `ownerLimits`: the owner's standing budget, diet, and place constraints, as a `ConstraintSet` the service reads when a friend asks.

The organizer's own limits arrive in `SkillIntent.rules`, as for every skill.

## 4. Lane A: consent events (settled by ADR 0011, amendment 15)

Every send names its interaction in `OutboundContext.interaction`, so the sheet finds it through `Disclosure.interaction` even before the coordinator has applied `.incoming`. The notes below are kept for the record.

- **What.** The service does not emit `consentNeeded` or `consentGiven`, because it cannot see when the Outbox asks. The coordinator should derive both from its `ConsentProvider`, using `Disclosure.conversation`.
- **A race to handle.** A friend's first send (its list) can ask for consent before the coordinator has applied the `.incoming` event that created the interaction. The sheet should wait for that interaction to appear. The test harness's `CoordinatorConsent` waits up to 1 second.
- **On decline.** When the owner declines, the service adds no event and stops quietly: the coordinator applies the pass (Orchestrator rule, 2026-10-01). A send the policy denies emits `.blockedByPrivacy` at any live step. Two tests rely on Core's pending change that accepts it from proposed and confirmed, and check the emitted event until it lands.
- **After a restart.** Open sheets are gone, and no service event can clear a pending consent ID. The coordinator should settle orphaned `pendingConsents` at launch.

## 5. Lane A: invitee participants and proposal cards

- **Participants.** For `.incoming(id, conversation:, from:, chainedFrom:)`, record `from` as the first of the invitee interaction's participants. `restore(_:)` uses it to know who organized a request that has no proposal yet.
- **Cards.** Show a Pick a place card with `PickAPlaceCopy.proposal(PickAPlaceCopy.facts(for: proposal, me:, nickname:, timeZone:), model:)`. It returns a headline and a detail line. Never pass a Pick a place `ProposalFacts` with `place` set to `SkillModel.proposalText` (ADR 0231).
- **Decide now (suggestion).** For a group at a table, the 15-minute answer window is long when nothing fits someone, because that friend stays silent (ADR 0230, decision 4). A shorter `PickAPlaceConfiguration.answerWindow`, or a later "Decide now" API, would fix it. Tell me which you want.

## 6. Lanes A and E: chaining "Somewhere else?"

- **The request.** Start Pick a place with:
  - `SkillRequest.inputs` holding the parent `.plan(...)`, or a `.timeSlot` from Find a time;
  - `participants` set to the plan's attendees without the owner;
  - `chainedFrom` set to the parent conversation.

  The proposal then carries the plan's time and activity, so every phone builds the same plan.
- **The result.** The service reports `.produced(.placeChoice)` and `.produced(.attendees)`, the people who said yes, which can be fewer than the proposal named. Updating the parent plan with `Plan.updating(place:)`, and its attendees, is the chain's job.

## 7. Lane B (StarlingAgent)

- **What.** `SkillModel.proposalText` must not render `ProposalFacts.place` into a prompt for `pick_a_place`. `PickAPlaceCopy` never passes it, but the implementation should drop it too. Keep "N place options" for `IssueValue.places`.
- **Why.** ADR 0231. A venue name is text a friend may choose.

## 8. Lane P15-F (red team): input on ADR 0231

ADR 0231 is Proposed until you review it. Scenarios worth adding:

- A friend probing an owner's limits with crafted candidate lists. Expect: silence when nothing fits, and a consent sheet whenever a list goes out.
- A real Maps identifier under a friendlier name. Expect: rejected by the friend's own lookup.
- A hostile venue name end to end. Expect: shown, never prompted.
- Floods of requests. Expect: 4 per friend, 32 live.
- A forged roster or confirmation. Expect: ignored.

Unit versions of each are in `Packages/Skills/PickAPlace/Tests/PickAPlaceTests/AdversarialTests.swift` and `VenueNameTests.swift`.

## 9. Orchestrator: THREAT_MODEL additions

- The organizer learns each friend's acceptable subset of its own candidates, in order. This is the aggregation's disclosure, and it is shown on the friend's consent sheet first.
- Apple Maps receives the owner's typed area, or a region around the owner for Nearby. On friends' phones it receives the identifiers of venues they are asked about.
- Silence hides "nothing fits" from the organizer, at the cost of waiting for the answer window.
- Hard limits are enforced only on known facts. Apple Maps supplies categories but no price or diet data, so on device most budget and diet limits are unchecked (ADR 0230).

## 10. Orchestrator (Core): a consent request cancelled, not answered

**Settled in Core v2.1 (PR #57)** as `consentCancelled(request:)`, applied by the coordinator. The rest of this item is kept for the record.

- **What.** An `InteractionEvent` for a consent request that was cancelled rather than approved or declined, for example `consentWithdrawn(request:)`. It should resume the interrupted step when it was the last open request, as `consentGiven` does.
- **Why.** The organizer asks several friends at once. When it decides, it cancels a query still waiting on its consent sheet, which keeps that query from reaching a friend already told "no plan". But the coordinator is still in `awaitingConsent(resume: .negotiating)`, so the `proposalReady` that follows is refused, and the owner never sees the card. The same happens to `everyoneConfirmed` if a propose to one friend is still on its sheet when the confirm window closes. Test: `InFlightTests.aDenialForASupersededStepIsDropped`, which answers the service directly to show the service side works.
- **Meanwhile.** The service is correct on its own. Lane A could dismiss a sheet whose send was cancelled and apply `consentGiven` for it, but that records an approval the owner never gave.

## 11. Orchestrator: smaller gaps, for the record

- **Chain after a restart.** An invitee `Interaction` has no field for the `chainedFrom` the request arrived with. After a restart, a friend's later proposals build a plan whose origin is the conversation, not the chain parent.
- **Ended requests across a restart.** Settled by ADR 0011 amendment 15: `restore(_:)` also gets interactions that ended in the last 24 hours, and the service keeps markers for them.
- **One conversation, two organizers.** Invites are keyed by conversation. A co-invitee who knows a conversation ID can send its own query on it to another friend before the real organizer's query arrives, and take the request over: that friend's list goes to the co-invitee. Keying by organizer and conversation would give one phone two interactions with the same conversation, which `InteractionStore.interaction(conversation:)` cannot hold. Lane F covers it as a scenario; the limit is recorded in ADR 0230.

## 12. Orchestrator (policy): a yes to a proposal under Never

**Settled by Core PR #58** (ADR 0019, amendment 10): every acceptance now passes the proposal in `OutboundContext.accepting`. The rest of this item is kept for the record.

- **What.** Consider letting the policy treat an `accept` whose terms equal the proposal it names as a yes or no, the way `OutboundContext.answering` lets an answer through.
- **Why.** With place set to Never, a friend's list still goes, because it is a yes or no to the organizer's own places. But the friend's yes to the proposal repeats the place, and people when the roster travels, so the policy denies it, and the friend cannot join a plan they said yes to. Test: `PrivacyTests.placeSetToNeverStillSaysYesOrNoButCannotAccept`.
- **Meanwhile.** The service reports the denial as `blockedByPrivacy` (amendment 14), and the request ends cleanly on both phones.
